Have you heard of the Finger Maidens?<br>

But you, I am afraid, are maidenless.<br>
I can play the role of maiden.<br>
Turning runes into strength.<br>
To aid you in your search for the Elden Ring.<br>
You need only take me with you.<br>
To the foot of the Erdtree.***

the principles you would follow.<br>***

You can see them, can't you?<br>
The rays of grace, that guide you through your burden.<br><br>
Me?<br>

For my purpose, given to me by my mother inside the Erdtree, long ago.<br>
For the reason that I yet live, burned and bodiless.<br>

I am no maiden. <br>
My purpose…was long ago lost.***

As an ally by pact,<br>

To face the challenge presented by the Ring.***

 I've been...testing you.<br>
To determine if the Elden Ring would truly have you.<br>
If you had the mettle to endure this long and arduous path.<br>
It seems my worries were unfounded.<br>
Torrent had your measure from the very start.<br>
Whereas I merely pretended.<br>

I can take you to the Roundtable Hold...<br>
Gathering place of Tarnished champions, guided by Grace...***

The reason for which I live, burned and bodiless.***

Farewell.<br>

> ***Hello again, old friend.<br>
It's me, Melina. Your travelling companion.<br>
Allow me a moment to converse with you.<br>
You were unable to enter the Erdtree, no?<br>
Prevented by the mantle of barbs.<br>
The thorns are impenetrable.<br>
A husk of the Erdtree's being; that spurns all that exists without.<br>
The only way to stand before the Elden Ring...<br>
and become the Elden Lord...is to pass the thorns.<br>
My purpose serves to aid in that very act.<br>
So I'd like you to undertake a new journey, with me.<br>
To the flame of ruin, far above the clouds, upon the snowy mountaintops of the giants.<br>
Then I can set the Erdtree aflame.<br>
And guide you. <br>
Down the path to becoming Elden Lord.***

> ***Think not, of the kindling.<br>
I shall see to that.<br>
All I ask of you is to make the journey.<br>
To the snowy mountaintops of the giants, far above the clouds.<br>
I know...<br>
I'm asking you to commit a cardinal sin.<br>
But it must be done to reach the path beyond.<br>
And that is the path I wish to travel.<br>
What is your mind?***

Not even you.***

Please, leave the frenzied flame alone.***

Please, seek not the frenzied flame.<br>

deny not the lives, the new births of this world.<br>
