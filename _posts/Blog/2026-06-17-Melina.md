> ***Greetings.<br>
Traveller from beyond the Fog.<br>
I am Melina.<br>
I offer you an accord.<br>
Have you heard of the Finger Maidens?<br>
They serve the Two Fingers, offering guidance, and aid, to the Tarnished.<br>
But you, I am afraid, are maidenless.<br>
I can play the role of maiden.<br>
Turning runes into strength.<br>
To aid you in your search for the Elden Ring.<br>
You need only take me with you.<br>
To the foot of the Erdtree.***

![](/assets/images/Blog/Melina/2.png)


> ***Shall I turn your runes to strength?<br>
Let my hand rest upon you, for but a moment.<br>
Share them with me,<br>
your thoughts,<br>
your ambitions,<br>
the principles you would follow.<br>***

> ***This tiny golden aura is the grace of the Erdtree.<br>
This light once shone in the eyes of your Tarnished brethren.<br>
But now it is all that guides you. Or so I hear.<br>
You can see them, can't you?<br>
The rays of grace, that guide you through your burden.<br><br>
Me?<br>
I'm searching.<br>
For my purpose, given to me by my mother inside the Erdtree, long ago.<br>
For the reason that I yet live, burned and bodiless.<br>
There is something for which I must apologise.<br>
I've acted the Finger Maiden, yet can offer no guidance.<br>
I am no maiden. <br>
My purpose…was long ago lost.***

If the rays of grace signal the castle,
then the Elden Ring beckons you.<br>
As an ally by pact,<br>

To face the challenge presented by the Ring.***
